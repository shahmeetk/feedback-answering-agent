# Start here (no coding needed)

This folder is an **AI agent** that helps you draft honest answers to feedback forms and surveys.

You stay in control. The agent **writes drafts**. **You** review them and submit the form yourself.

---

## What can it help with?

| Situation | Example |
|---|---|
| Product feedback | App, website, or tool survey |
| Person feedback | Peer review, 360 review, recommendation |
| Service feedback | Support call, delivery, appointment |
| Experience feedback | Event, course, hotel stay |
| Any survey link | Google Form, Typeform, company survey |

---

## Pick how you feel

Tell the agent one of these moods (called **sentiment**):

| You say… | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Positive** | Mostly good; warm and helpful |
| **Negative** | Something went wrong; clear and fair |
| **Moderate** | Mixed; balanced |
| **Extremely happy** | Loved it; enthusiastic |
| **Extremely sad** | Very disappointed; still respectful |

The agent will **not invent** experiences. It only uses what you tell it.

---

## 3 steps (any AI tool)

1. **Open this folder** in Cursor, Claude, Claude Code, Antigravity, or another AI coding/chat tool.  
   Or open a normal chat (Claude.ai, ChatGPT) and attach the files listed below.
2. **Tell the agent your story** — what happened, how you feel, and the questions (or paste the survey link / screenshots).
3. **Copy the suggested answers** into the real form after you check them.

Copy-paste starter message:

```text
You are my Feedback Answering Agent.
Read START_HERE.md, AGENTS.md, and prompts/feedback-agent.md.
I need help drafting feedback. Do not submit anything.

Subject: [what or who this is about]
Type: [person / product / service / experience / survey]
Sentiment: [positive / negative / moderate / extremely_happy / extremely_sad]
My experience:
- [fact 1]
- [fact 2]
Questions (or paste the survey / screenshots):
1. ...
2. ...
```
